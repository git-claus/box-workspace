Privacy policy

box workspace is a private tool used only by its owner, with one Google account that the owner controls. No one else signs in to it.

What it accesses

With the owner's consent it uses these Google permissions on that one account: Gmail (to send messages to the owner, create drafts, and read incoming verification codes and messages the owner sends himself), Google Calendar events, and Google Drive files that the tool itself creates (Docs and Sheets).

How the data is used and stored

Data is used only to carry out the owner's own requests. Short extracts (such as a one-time code) are kept on the owner's private server for a few hours at most. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train models.

Revoking access

Access can be withdrawn at any time from the Google account's security settings (Third-party apps with account access).
